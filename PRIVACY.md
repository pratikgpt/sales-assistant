# Privacy policy: Sales Assistant demo

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

Sales Assistant is a prototype built for a hiring assignment. It answers enquiries for **Quillfield Analytics**,
a fictional analytics consultancy. It is not a commercial service and is used only with test accounts.

## What is collected

When you message the connected WhatsApp number, Instagram account or email inbox, the app stores:

- your messages and the replies sent to you;
- the identifier the channel provides (phone number, Instagram-scoped user id and username, or email address) and
  the display name you share;
- details you give about your enquiry (company, service needed, budget, timeline) and any meeting booked.

## How it is used

Only to reply to your enquiry, decide whether it fits the demo business's criteria, assign it to a (fictional) sales
representative and book a test meeting in a test calendar. Message text is sent to a language model provider
(via OpenRouter) to understand the enquiry and draft replies. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising or used for
any other purpose.

## Storage and retention

Data is stored in the app's database and kept only for the duration of the assignment review, after which it is
deleted.

## Deleting your data

Send the message "delete my data" on the channel you used (WhatsApp, Instagram DM or email), or open an issue on
this repository. Your messages, contact record and any booking will be removed.
